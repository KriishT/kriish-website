## The problem

Scammers go after older adults with romance, gift-card, IRS and “grandparent” scams, and now with deepfakes and crypto schemes too. DART Academy teaches people to recognise them through short, interactive courses. Its learners are older adults, so the interface can’t lean on the habits most apps take for granted.

## My role

I worked on DART twice, from two sides.

- **Frontend engineering intern, Sep 2024 to Aug 2025.** I built the learning interfaces in React and Next.js, turned the UI designs into Tailwind components, built bulk enrolment, and built real-time presenter-note sync.
- **UX researcher, Jan 2026 to now.** I ran usability research with 60+ older adults and turned what I found into 12 issues and recommendations.
