## The problem

Scammers go after older adults with romance, gift-card, IRS and “grandparent” scams, and now with deepfakes and crypto schemes too. DART Academy teaches people to recognise them through short, interactive courses. Its learners are older adults, so the interface can’t lean on the habits most apps take for granted.

## My role

I worked on DART twice, from two sides.

- **Frontend engineering intern, Sep 2024 to Aug 2025.** I built the learning interfaces in React and Next.js, built bulk enrolment, and built real-time presenter-note sync.
- **UX researcher, Jan 2026 to now.** I ran usability research with older adults and turned what I found into 12 issues and recommendations.

The team was two people. I designed the screens and I built them. We met every week, and each meeting was a deadline.

## Process

Our learners aren’t tech savvy, so most of the work went into making the site very easy to use: clear, familiar screens that don’t assume anyone has used an app like this before. The weekly meetings kept the work honest. Each week there was something to show, and we judged it by whether an older learner could use it without help.

## The browse page

I designed the browse page, where learners choose what to learn, on my own.

Every course says its level, its length and how long it takes before anyone opens it." />

It’s one grid of courses. Each card shows a picture, a level tag (1 for lessons, 2 for practice, 3 for evaluations), how many chapters or questions it has and roughly how long it will take. Someone can see what a course asks of them before they start.

## Decisions and tradeoffs

### Enrolling a whole class from a spreadsheet

Administrators enrol groups of learners by importing a CSV, and every row is validated in real time before anything is written.

I chose a spreadsheet over an invite form because administrators usually already have their learners listed in one. A class of thirty is one upload, where a form means thirty trips through the same screen. Checking each row as it’s read means a typo is flagged before anything is saved, so the administrator fixes it rather than finding it later.

## Craft details

- Course modules for romance, gift-card, IRS, grandparent, password, deepfake and crypto scams.
- Account, accessibility, privacy and terms pages.
- An admin dashboard for learner analytics and announcements.
- Presenter notes that stay in sync between the presentation and the speaker’s view, in real time.

## Outcome

- The platform serves 1,000+ older adults.
- Usability research with 60+ older adults surfaced 12 issues, each turned into a recommendation.
